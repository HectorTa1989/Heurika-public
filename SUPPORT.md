# Support

## Getting Help

If you need help with Heurika, here are the best ways to get support:

## Documentation

### Quick Start Guide
1. **Install Heurika** from the App Store
2. **Enable the keyboard** in Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → Heurika
3. **Grant Full Access** to enable AI features (optional but recommended)
4. **Start typing** - hold the Heurika Bar when you want AI assistance

### Common Issues

#### Keyboard Not Appearing
- Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards
- Ensure Heurika is enabled
- Make sure "Allow Full Access" is enabled for AI features

#### AI Features Not Working
- Check that you have an active subscription
- Verify your AI provider API key is configured in Settings
- Ensure you have internet connection
- Check that AI data consent is enabled in Settings

#### Notion Integration Not Working
- Verify your Notion API key is correct
- Ensure the integration has proper permissions in Notion
- Check your internet connection

## FAQ

**Q: Is Heurika free?**
A: Heurika offers a free trial. After the trial, a subscription is required for premium features.

**Q: Does Heurika collect my data?**
A: Heurika only sends data to AI providers when you explicitly trigger an action. You can disable AI features at any time. See our Privacy Policy for details.

**Q: Which AI providers does Heurika support?**
A: Heurika supports OpenAI, Anthropic, and Groq. You can configure your preferred provider in Settings.

**Q: Can I use Heurika without AI features?**
A: Yes, Heurika functions as a standard keyboard even without AI features enabled.

**Q: Is my text sent to AI providers?**
A: Only when you explicitly trigger an action by holding the Heurika Bar. Regular typing is not sent to AI providers.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Cancel through Apple's subscription settings: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Heurika.
