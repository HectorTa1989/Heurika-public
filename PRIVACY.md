# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 21, 2026**

## Overview

Heurika is an AI-powered keyboard extension that helps you type more efficiently by understanding your intent and taking actions on your behalf. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal information.

## Information We Collect

### Data Processed Locally
- **Text Input**: Your typed text is processed locally on your device for basic keyboard functionality
- **Keyboard State**: Layout preferences and settings are stored locally on your device

### Data Sent to AI Providers (With Your Consent)
When you explicitly trigger a "Deed" (AI action), Heurika may send the following to AI providers:
- **The text you've typed** in the current context
- **Your intent** (e.g., "search for", "create calendar event")
- **Context** needed to fulfill your request

This only happens when you:
- Hold the Heurika Bar to trigger an action
- Have explicitly granted AI data consent in the app settings

### Data We Do Not Collect
- We do not collect your passwords or sensitive authentication information
- We do not log your keystrokes for analytics
- We do not sell your personal data to third parties

## AI Data Consent

You have full control over whether AI features are enabled:
- AI features are **opt-in** and require explicit consent
- You can disable AI features at any time in Settings
- When disabled, the keyboard functions as a standard keyboard without AI capabilities

## Data Storage

- **Local Storage**: App settings and preferences are stored on your device
- **Cloud Services**: When you use AI features, data is sent to your chosen AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Groq) according to their privacy policies
- **Notion Integration**: If you connect Notion, we store your API key securely in your device's keychain

## Third-Party Services

Heurika integrates with:
- **AI Providers**: OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq - for processing AI requests
- **Notion**: For task and note integration
- **RevenueCat**: For subscription management

Each service has its own privacy policy, which you should review.

## Data Security

- API keys are stored securely in your device's keychain
- All network communications use HTTPS encryption
- We follow Apple's security guidelines for keyboard extensions

## Children's Privacy

Heurika is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your data
- Delete your data
- Disable AI features
- Revoke third-party service connections
- Request a copy of your data
